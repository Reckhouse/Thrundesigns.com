# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary buyers are founders and owners of small businesses — including startups and companies undergoing a rebrand — who need strategic design help to present themselves clearly and move a project forward.

## Product Purpose

Thrun Design Co. is a strategic design studio. This product is the studio’s public marketing site: it explains the offer, shows work, outlines process, and converts qualified visitors into project inquiries through a quote request flow.

Success means the right founders/owners understand the offer quickly and submit a clear project quote.

## Positioning

Thrun Design Co. partners with small businesses on brand and system architecture, web design and maintenance, business marketing audits, and print and digital assets — cohesive systems rather than one-off decoration.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate the studio from a marketing site (homepage, work, process) and request work through a multi-step quote form. Studio operators edit site content in Sanity Studio and receive quote submissions (with optional file attachments) from the live site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

## Product Principles

1. Speak to founders and owners who are ready to rebrand, launch, or tighten their system — not to design-industry insiders alone.
2. Lead with systems and outcomes (brand architecture, web, audits, assets), not decorative novelty.
3. Keep the path to a project quote short, clear, and credible.
4. Treat portfolio and proof as provisional until real work replaces concepts — never invent social proof.
5. Preserve the Thrun Design Co. name and existing brand mark across surfaces.
